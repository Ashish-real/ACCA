🧮 The Maths — Total Papers

13.3
Applied Skills Level
6 Papers — The Technical Core

This is the heart of ACCA — six papers covering law, performance management, tax, financial reporting, audit, and financial management. Most students find this the most demanding level.

PaperFocusConnects to Bible ChapterExam Format
LW — Corporate & Business LawLegal systems, company law, contract law, employment lawNew topic — covered later in BibleCBE, 2 hours, MCQs + scenarios

TX — TaxationIncome tax, corporation tax, VAT/GST, capital gainsTaxation Fundamentals chapter (later)CBE, 3 hours, constructed response
FR — Financial ReportingFull IFRS application, consolidation, interpretationCh 3-4, 7-12 (Financial Statements through Interpretation)CBE, 3 hours, constructed response
AA — Audit & AssuranceAudit planning, procedures, evidence, reportingAudit Fundamentals chapter (later)CBE, 3 hours, constructed response
FM — Financial ManagementInvestment appraisal, working capital, cost of capital, risk managementCorporate Finance chapter (later)CBE, 3 hours, constructed response
🎯 Recommended Order — Applied Skills
Most students follow: LW → PM → TX → FR → FM → AA (or FR before TX/FM, depending on local guidance). FR is the cornerstone — it builds directly on FA and feeds into SBR. PM builds on MA. Many ACCA tuition providers recommend grouping FR + FM together as they share corporate finance concepts.
13.4
Strategic Professional Level
4 Papers — Think Like a CFO

The final level transforms you from a technically competent accountant into a strategic business advisor. Both compulsory papers are essential — and your choice of 2 options should align with your career direction.

🧭
SBL — Strategic Business Leader
  • Combines old P1 (Governance, Risk, Ethics) + P3 (Business Analysis)
  • Case study format — analyse a fictional organisation
  • Tests strategy, leadership, governance, risk, technology
  • 4-hour exam — the longest ACCA paper
Compulsory
📊
SBR — Strategic Business Reporting
  • Advanced IFRS — group accounts, complex transactions
  • Builds directly on FR and Chapters 7-11 of this Bible
  • Ethics in financial reporting
  • Current issues in corporate reporting
Compulsory
💰
AFM — Advanced Financial Management
  • For: Investment banking, treasury, corporate finance career paths
  • Advanced valuation, M&A, risk management, treasury
  • Builds on FM
  • Most quantitative of the options
Option — Recommended for Finance Track
📈
APM — Advanced Performance Management
  • For: FP&A, strategy, consulting career paths
  • Advanced management accounting, strategic performance systems
  • Builds on PM
  • Heavy on Balanced Scorecard, performance frameworks
Option — Recommended for FP&A Track
🧾
ATX — Advanced Taxation
  • For: Tax advisory, Big 4 tax practice career paths
  • Complex tax planning, international tax
  • Builds on TX
  • Highly specialised — Big 4 tax teams value this
Option — Recommended for Tax Track
🔍
AAA — Advanced Audit & Assurance
  • For: External audit, Big 4 audit partner track
  • Complex audit scenarios, group audits, ethics
  • Builds on AA
  • Essential for audit career progression
Option — Recommended for Audit Track
🎯 Choosing Your Options — Career Alignment
Want Big 4 Audit Partner track? → AAA + APM or AFM
Want Investment Banking / Treasury? → AFM + APM
Want FP&A / CFO track? → APM + AFM
Want Tax Specialist (Big 4 Tax)? → ATX + AAA or APM

Most students choose AFM + APM for maximum flexibility — these are the most broadly applicable to corporate finance and FP&A roles, which align well with the MNC career path covered in Chapter 1.
13.5
Ethics and Professional Skills Module (EPSM)

EPSM is a mandatory online module — not a written exam — that must be completed before applying for ACCA membership. It develops the professional skills employers value most.

⚖️
Ethics Component
  • Interactive scenarios on ethical dilemmas
  • Based on ACCA's Code of Ethics and Conduct
  • Links to real workplace situations
  • Builds on the fundamental principles: Integrity, Objectivity, Competence, Confidentiality, Professional Behaviour
🛠️
Professional Skills Component
  • Communication, commercial acumen, analysis
  • Innovation, problem-solving, leadership
  • Self-paced, interactive online learning
  • Best taken alongside Strategic Professional papers
13.6
Practical Experience Requirement (PER)

ACCA is not just about exams. To become a member (not just qualified), you need 36 months of relevant practical experience, achieving specific performance objectives.

📅
36 Months Requirement
  • Can be completed before, during, or after exams
  • Must be in an accounting/finance-related role
  • Recorded via ACCA's online "My Experience" tool
  • Internships and articleship can count
🎯
Performance Objectives
  • 9 Essentials (all candidates must achieve)
  • Plus Technical objectives matching your role
  • Signed off by a workplace mentor
  • Demonstrates real-world application of skills
💼
Big 4 Articleship Advantage
  • Big 4 firms structure roles to map directly to PER objectives
  • Most students complete PER during their first job
  • Starting early (internships during studies) accelerates this

Exemptions — How Your Degree Helps

ACCA grants exemptions for certain papers based on prior qualifications — most relevantly, your B.Com degree (as planned in your roadmap).

Prior QualificationTypical ExemptionsPapers Still Required
B.Com (Indian universities — varies)Often BT, MA, FA (up to 3 papers)Remaining 9-10 papers
B.Com + Specific accredited programsUp to 9 papers in some cases (rare)4 papers
No prior qualification (after Class 12)NoneAll 13 papers
CA / CMA (India)Significant exemptions — varies by qualification levelVaries — often just SBL, SBR + 1 option
⚠️ Important — Exemptions Are a Trade-Off
Exemptions save TIME and MONEY — but you skip the structured learning for those papers. Since this Bible covers BT, MA, and FA concepts in depth (Chapters 1-6), even if you claim exemptions, you'll still have the underlying knowledge. Many top performers choose NOT to claim exemptions for foundational papers — building strong fundamentals pays off at Strategic Professional level. This is a personal decision — discuss with your ACCA tuition provider.

Registration, Fees & Exam Logistics
📝
Registration
  • Register at accaglobal.com — create MyACCA account
  • Initial registration fee (one-time)
  • Annual subscription fee (recurring)
  • Exam fees per paper — vary by level and booking window
💻
Computer-Based Exams (CBE)
  • Applied Knowledge — on-demand, any time
  • Applied Skills + Strategic Professional — 4 exam sessions per year (March, June, Sept, Dec)
  • Taken at Pearson VUE test centres
  • Early booking = lower fees
💰
Total Cost Estimate (India)
  • Registration + subscription: ~₹15,000-20,000 total over course
  • Exam fees: ~₹8,000-15,000 per paper (varies by booking time)
  • Total exam fees for 13 papers: ~₹1.5-2.5 lakh
  • Tuition (BPP/Kaplan/local): ~₹50,000-1,50,000 per level

13.11
Common Mistakes, Flashcards & Interview Q&A

Part A — Common Mistakes (Don't Repeat These)

❌ MISTAKE 1
Ignoring the Recommended Paper Order
While ACCA allows flexibility in exam order (within level constraints), skipping the logical progression (e.g. attempting SBR before FR, or FM before understanding basic accounting) makes each paper significantly harder, since later papers build directly on earlier ones.
❌ MISTAKE 2
Choosing Strategic Professional Options Without Career Alignment
Picking AFM and ATX together, for example, sends mixed signals about career direction (corporate finance vs tax specialism). Options should align with your intended career path — audit, tax, corporate finance, or FP&A — as recruiters do notice this combination.
❌ MISTAKE 3
Leaving EPSM and PER Until the Very End
EPSM (Ethics and Professional Skills Module) can be started as soon as you reach Applied Skills level, and PER (Practical Experience Requirement) hours can be logged as you gain relevant work experience — both can run in parallel with exams rather than being left as a bottleneck after your last paper.
❌ MISTAKE 4
Not Checking Exemptions Before Registering
Many students with relevant degrees are eligible for exemptions from some Applied Knowledge or even Applied Skills papers. Registering and simply attempting all 13 papers without checking exemption eligibility wastes both time and exam fees.
❌ MISTAKE 5
Underestimating the Jump from Applied Skills to Strategic Professional
Strategic Professional papers, especially SBL, require a fundamentally different skill — applying technical knowledge to unstructured, real-world case studies with time pressure — not just recalling and calculating. Students who only revised technical content without practising case-study technique often underperform at this level.

Part C — Interview Questions & Model Answers

QuestionWhat They Are TestingModel Answer
"Why did you choose ACCA over other qualifications, and what stage are you at?"Career planning clarity and self-awarenessACCA offers global recognition, flexibility to combine exams with work experience, and a syllabus that covers both technical accounting and strategic business skills — well suited to an international finance career. I'd state my current level clearly (e.g. "I've completed Applied Knowledge and am progressing through Applied Skills") and connect it to a concrete timeline for completion.
"Which Strategic Professional options are you planning to take, and why?"Career direction and forward planningA strong answer names specific options (e.g. AFM and APM) and explicitly ties them to a career goal — for example, "I'm choosing AFM because I'm interested in corporate finance and treasury roles, where advanced valuation and risk management skills are directly applicable."
"How do you manage studying for ACCA while working / studying full-time?"Time management and commitmentA credible answer describes a concrete system — a fixed weekly study schedule, spaced revision using past papers, and realistic pacing (e.g. one paper per exam session) — rather than vague claims of "working hard." Mentioning specific resources (OpenTuition, ACCA's own study hub, past paper banks) adds credibility.
