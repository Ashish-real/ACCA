What is ACCA?

The Association of Chartered Certified Accountants (ACCA) is the global body for professional accountants. Recognized in over 180 countries, the qualification validates expertise in financial accounting, management reporting, taxation, audit, corporate finance, and executive leadership.

Landing a Big 4 Job

The Big 4 accounting firms (PwC, Deloitte, EY, KPMG) actively recruit ACCA qualified professionals and candidates. This 50-chapter guide equips you with both the theoretical exam knowledge and practical analytical skills (Excel, Power BI, financial modelling) expected in technical Big 4 interviews.
