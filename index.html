01 / THE IDEA

What is ACCA?

The Association of Chartered Certified Accountants (ACCA) is the world's leading body for professional finance & accounting practitioners. Recognized in 180+ countries, ACCA prepares financial managers, auditors, tax specialists, and corporate strategists.

Landing a Big 4 Job

This 50-chapter guide specifically equips candidates with both the exam theory and practical tools (Excel, Power BI, Financial Modelling) required to clear technical Big 4 interviews at PwC, Deloitte, EY, and KPMG.
