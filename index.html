📜 About the Finance Career Bible

The Finance Career Bible is a comprehensive 50-chapter curriculum engineered to take learners from absolute zero in accounting to mastering complex financial analysis, IFRS reporting, valuation models, FP&A forecasting, and Strategic Professional ACCA papers.

Why This Book Was Created

Traditional finance textbooks are often fragmented—either focusing purely on academic theory or isolated exam papers. This project bridges the gap by synthesizing 13 ACCA papers into a continuous, real-world finance career roadmap.

Structure Overview

  • Foundations (Ch 1-12): Accounting basics, IFRS standards, revenue, inventory, PPE.
  • ACCA Journey (Ch 13-17): Exam strategies, qualification map, ethics.
  • Analysis & Modelling (Ch 18-22): Ratio analysis, FP&A, DCF valuation, financial models.
  • Advanced ACCA & Tech (Ch 23-48): AFM, APM, SBL, Power BI, SQL, Python for finance, ESG.
  • Exam Tools (Ch 49-50): Master formula cheat-sheet and 44+ practice question bank.
