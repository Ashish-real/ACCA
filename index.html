Regulatory & Standard-Setting Bodies

  • IFRS Foundation (ifrs.org) — Official IFRS and ISSB standards — primary source, always more reliable than secondary summaries.
  • ACCA Global (accaglobal.com) — Official ACCA resources, technical articles, study guides, and CPD content.
  • SEBI (sebi.gov.in) — All Indian securities regulation, corporate circulars, and BRSR sustainability framework updates.
  • RBI (rbi.org.in) — Monetary policy, banking regulation, interest rate decisions, and CBDC digital rupee updates.
  • MCA (mca.gov.in) — Companies Act 2013, corporate statutory filings, and MCA21 portal compliance.
  • ICAI (icai.org) — Standards on Auditing (SAs) and technical guidance notes for chartered accountants.
📊 Market & Financial Data

Financial Data & Corporate Filings

  • NSE & BSE India (nseindia.com) — Audited company annual reports, quarterly filings, corporate actions, and credit ratings.
  • Screener.in (screener.in) — Clean 10-year historical financial data, ratio screening, and customized financial metrics.
  • Trendlyne (trendlyne.com) — Earnings call transcripts, analyst concalls, insider trading reports, and DII/FII changes.
  • TradingView (tradingview.com) — Free technical chart analytics, financial metrics, and multi-asset price feeds.
  • Bloomberg & Reuters — Global benchmark market data, macroeconomic indicators, sovereign bond yield curves.
🎓 Learning Hubs

Free Learning & Valuation Reference

  • Aswath Damodaran's Website (pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar) — Free valuation data, spreadsheets, Damodaran risk premiums, discount rates — extraordinary free resource.
  • Investopedia (investopedia.com) — Best general finance term reference and intuitive concept explanations.
  • CFI (corporatefinanceinstitute.com) — Free articles and templates on financial modelling, valuation, and Excel shortcuts.
  • OpenTuition (opentuition.com) — Free ACCA study notes, lectures, and active forums globally — extremely popular with ACCA students.
  • Harvard Business Review (hbr.org) — Leadership, strategy, corporate governance, and executive management thinking.

02 / THE METHODOLOGY

The 3 Gaps in Traditional Learning.

Gap 1: Theory vs Excel Modeling

Textbooks teach debit/credit theory on paper, but employers demand dynamic 3-statement Excel models with scenario toggles and debt schedules.

FCB Solution: Direct model walkthroughs in Chapters 21, 33, and 39.

Gap 2: Passing Exams vs Technical Interviews

Scoring 60% in SBR/AFM does not guarantee you can answer a partner's question on enterprise value vs equity value under pressure.

FCB Solution: Big 4 technical interview blueprints in Chapters 43, 44, and 45.

Gap 3: Siloed Paper Modules

Students study FA, FM, and TX as separate silos without realizing tax affects WACC, which dictates DCF valuation and impairment tests.

FCB Solution: Integrated 5-Pillar ecosystem showing cross-paper interplay.

04 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Student & Professional Guidance.

Who is Finance Career Bible designed for? +

It is designed for students starting after Class 12, university undergraduates studying B.Com alongside ACCA, working professionals clearing Strategic Professional papers, and finance aspirants preparing for technical analyst interviews at PwC, Deloitte, EY, KPMG, or boutique investment banks.

How does this Bible map to official ACCA papers? +

Every official syllabus area across all 13 ACCA papers (Applied Knowledge, Applied Skills, Strategic Professional, and Options) is mapped directly to its corresponding chapter in our Interactive Syllabus Timeline Matrix.

Do I still need Kaplan or BPP Exam Kits to pass formal ACCA exams? +

Yes. The Finance Career Bible builds first-principles comprehension, mental models, and real-world deal mechanics. For formal exam sitting, candidates must pair this understanding with an approved Kaplan or BPP Exam Kit and the official ACCA CBE Practice Platform for high-volume timed question practice.

How exactly is my reading progress, timers, and quiz scores tracked? +

Your learning progress is tracked 100% locally in your browser storage (via localStorage) using our smart 4-point verification engine:

  • 1. Active Dwell Timer: Tracks your active reading time (5-minute dwell required per chapter).
  • 2. Scroll Depth Tracker: Verifies how far down the chapter page you read (85% scroll required).
  • 3. Auto-Completion Badges: Once both criteria are met, the chapter is marked as Completed (Green Checkmark) across all portal indices and TOC drawers.
  • 4. Session Recovery: If you refresh (F5) or return days later, your active chapter, exact reading position, and active tab are automatically restored without any login or account creation.
Can I use this material offline? +

Yes. The entire repository is self-contained HTML5, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. Once downloaded or cloned, every chapter, formula, and practice question operates 100% offline.

How does the Bible prepare me for Big 4 technical partner interviews? +

Chapters 43 through 48 contain specialized Big 4 interview blueprints, audit workpapers, M&A due diligence cases, and technical partner round Q&A models used across PwC, Deloitte, EY, and KPMG.

How should I pace my study through the 50 chapters? +

Follow the interactive 5-Stage Roadmap tab. Completing 2 to 3 chapters per week alongside active exam preparation allows full curriculum coverage and practical modeling mastery within 4 to 6 months.
